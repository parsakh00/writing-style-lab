# Contributing

This skill is a first version and it gets better through use. The most useful
contribution is a report of writing that still reads as machine-generated or as
robotic formality after the skill has been applied: the passage, and which part of it
reads that way. The skill's job is then to find the construction behind that
impression and measure it against the papers, and a rule comes out of it with its
count.

Also welcome: a rule you have already measured, with its rate in published papers and
in drafts; a reference profile built from another field's papers or another group's
with `scripts/build_skill_reference.py`; and a correction to any target or claim in
SKILL.md that your own corpus contradicts, with what you measured and on how many words.

Any idea for making the skill better is welcome, whether or not it comes with a
measurement: a construction you suspect, a source worth adding, a field it should
cover, a way the output could be clearer. Open an issue or a pull request. If you use
the skill through an agent, it will check your report against the data and offer to
open the issue for you.
